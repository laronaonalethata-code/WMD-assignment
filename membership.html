Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a contract?

No! All our memberships are month-to-month. You can cancel anytime with 30 days notice.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes, you can freeze your membership for up to 3 months per year for medical or travel reasons.

Do you offer day passes?

Yes, day passes are available for $15. It's a great way to try out the gym before committing to a membership.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and bank transfers. Payments are processed automatically each month.
